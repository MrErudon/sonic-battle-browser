# M01.4 - CPU opponent

## Baseline and architecture

Based on updated main `c619ebc` (merged PR #3). No movement, combat physics,
fighter statistics, hitboxes or renderer changes were needed.

`src/core/cpu.ts` exposes `CpuController.tick(self, target): CombatInput`.
`ArenaScene` invokes it immediately before each `stepCombat`, inside the existing
60 Hz accumulator. Render frames do not advance AI timers. Inputs are generated
from pre-tick fighter state; P1's current input is not exposed to the CPU.
The controller never mutates fighters, sets facing directly, teleports, changes
health, bypasses recovery, or changes speed/damage by difficulty.

Pursuit evaluates the eight legal keyboard directions using a copy of locomotion
state and the existing `stepFighter`. This applies the exact inverse isometric
projection, normalized speed, air control and arena clamping. It selects the
step that reduces arena-space distance most; blocked directions cannot beat an
improving direction. Stable direction order breaks ties deterministically.
Spacing has a stop radius and a larger resume radius, avoiding repeated switching
at the threshold. Facing/alignment corrections use movement presses, including
very close targets. Attack eligibility reuses the oriented arena-space hitbox and
vertical overlap check, with an additional forward-facing check.

Initial movement waits for the reaction delay. Steering then tracks the current
position each simulation tick; attack reactions require continuous range/alignment
for the reaction duration. Attacks, cooldown, landing and hitstun suppress all
CPU inputs and rearm the reaction delay. An independent minimum attack interval
prevents immediate retries even if combat readiness changes. Probabilistic pauses
and light/heavy choices use a local Mulberry32 generator; seed zero is valid.
Grounded jump+attack uses the same legal simultaneous press available to P1.
KO of either fighter stops CPU input. R and difficulty selection recreate the
controller with seed 1 and reset both fighters. Scene recreation preserves the
selected difficulty and displays it correctly.

## Provisional parameters

All durations are integer simulation frames (60 frames = one second), distances
are arena units, and chances are probabilities per eligible decision. These are
prototype values, not original Sonic Battle frame data.

| Parameter | Easy | Normal | Hard |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| reactionFrames | 24 | 12 | 5 |
| attackInterval (minimum between emitted attacks) | 70 | 40 | 24 |
| pauseChance | 0.45 | 0.20 | 0.05 |
| pauseFrames | 45 | 24 | 12 |
| heavyChance | 0.10 | 0.35 | 0.50 |
| aerialChance | 0.02 | 0.08 | 0.15 |
| spacing | 44 | 44 | 44 |
| spacingSlack | 12 | 10 | 8 |

Construct a controller with a custom `CpuConfig` and numeric seed to tune behavior.
Configuration is copied/frozen and rejects negative/nonfinite values, invalid
frame counts, and probabilities outside [0,1]. Attack selection falls back to
the other attack if only that attack currently overlaps. Aerial chance applies
only on the ground. Reaction, pauses and legal recovery can make actual intervals
longer than `attackInterval`.

## Limits and future work

- Reactive pursuit, not tactical planning: no guard, dodge, combos, prediction,
  obstacles/pathfinding, retreat strategy or opponent-input reading.
- Spacing is an approach stop/resume band, not collision separation; fighters may
  overlap and the CPU does not backpedal to restore a minimum distance.
- Attack geometry is checked at decision time. Startup and target motion can
  cause misses. Random aerial attacks can miss as the fighter rises.
- Steering is immediate after the initial delay; configurable reaction delays
  govern initiation, attack commitment and recovery, not delayed visual sensing.
- Presets are deterministic and visibly different, but not balance-tested for
  competitive play. Current tests compare fixed-seed long-run cadence.
- Original procedural robots and impact effects remain. Close-range diagnostic
  labels can overlap; the existing large Phaser production chunk remains.
- No victory screen or automatic rematch; press R after KO.
