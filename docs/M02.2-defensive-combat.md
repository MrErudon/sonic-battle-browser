# M02.2 — Defensive combat

## Baseline and scope
Based on main `4ee789df71d88b362977a72d230e40ce3bbcb7ea`, merged PR #7.
M02.1 human gameplay/balance testing remains pending. This adds ground guard,
not healing, special counters, parries, dodging or invulnerability.
All defensive tuning is proposed prototype data, not measured GBA behavior.
Chat 02's available guidance leaves exact defensive timing unverified and
proposes 75% damage reduction. Repository-recorded Chat 03 requirements call
for replaceable original effects, ground anchors, depth sorting and simulation-owned

No Cloudflare configuration, hosting audience or deployment is changed.

## Controls and state contract
Hold L to block, next to J/K. Face the rival before raising guard.
Guard is stationary, locks facing, and takes precedence over simultaneous neutral
attack/jump input. Movement, jump and attack edges during defense are discarded.
It cannot cancel any part of an attack, hitstun, cooldown, landing or airborne state.
A held L can start defense on the first eligible tick after those states end.

- Ready -> Block Startup: frame 1 begins on the accepting tick.
- Startup frames 1–3 are vulnerable. Frame 4 becomes Blocking before contact resolution.
- Blocking lasts while held; a block enters Blockstun (6 light / 10 heavy ticks).
- Blockstun still blocks incoming frontal contacts. Its final tick returns to
  Blocking if held, otherwise starts Block Recovery.
- Releasing during startup or Blocking starts six full subsequent recovery ticks.
  The final recovery tick discards all control and becomes Ready at its end;
  a new action can begin on the following tick.
- Guard exhaustion starts a fixed 36-subsequent-tick break timer. No guard,
  voluntary movement, jump or attack is allowed during that interval.
- The breaking contact deals chip; follow-up hits deal ordinary damage/hitstun.
  Hits never restart the break timer. At expiry, guard refills to maximum.
  Any ordinary hitstun/airborne/landing/cooldown lock still completes.
  If ordinary hitstun ends before the break timer, Guard Broken resumes.
- Help/blur clears held defense intent and moves an existing stance into recovery,
  without advancing time while paused. Recovery runs after resume. L must be
  released/repressed; repeat events cannot rearm it.
- KO and match transitions clear defensive action/timers; round/menu/rematch
  restore full guard. Existing KO hitstun/attack poses remain otherwise governed
  by the match controller. Scene listener cleanup is unchanged.

## Provisional configuration (60 ticks/second)
`FighterStats.defense` optionally overrides `DEFAULT_DEFENSE`.
Durations are positive integers; probabilities/ratios are finite and bounded;
guard capacity/costs are positive; regeneration may be zero.

| Parameter | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Startup / release recovery | 3 / 6 ticks |
| Maximum guard | 100 |
| Chip ratio | .25, rounded up, clamped to remaining HP; lethal |
| Blocked horizontal knockback | .25 of attack; no launch |
| Guard break | 36 ticks |
| Regeneration delay | 60 ticks after guard/contact |
| Regeneration rate | .5 per tick, grounded Ready only |
| Light / heavy blockstun | 6 / 10 ticks |
| Light 1 / 2 / 3 guard cost | 18 / 22 / 26 |
| Standalone heavy / finisher cost | 45 |
| Air light / heavy guard cost | 25 / 35 |

Holding startup, block, blockstun, recovery or break prevents regeneration.
Normal received hits also delay it. Defaults leave an escape gap between
uncancelled blocked attacks, including at walls. Blockstun configuration is
bounded to 6/10 ticks to preserve that guarantee for existing attack tuning;
new attack timings need a corresponding review of safe duration bounds.

## Geometry, combos and simultaneous contact
Only grounded guard can defend. All current attacks, including aerial contacts,
are blockable in the front 180 degrees; side boundary counts as front.
Rear contacts and exactly coincident fighter origins bypass guard.
Existing arena-space rectangle/circle and vertical overlap rules still apply.
Blocking never creates contacts outside the existing attack volume.

An attack owns consumed contacts separately from damage confirmations.
A block consumes its one contact but never confirms a combo cancel. Block and
guard-break chip never increase the consecutive-hit HUD. Blocked stages run
normal recovery/cooldown and cannot continue a chain. Successful unblocked
M02.1 hits retain their timing, six-tick buffer, damage and continuation rules.

Contacts and block eligibility are collected before any reaction is applied.
Two fighters' simultaneous active attacks still trade, even with block held,
because existing attacks cannot transition into guard. Reverse fighter-order
tests and additional simultaneous guard contacts cover resolution.
Existing single-player two-fighter scope is retained; this is not a new
authoritative multiplayer/multi-attacker resolution design.

## CPU
New per-preset parameters:

| Difficulty | Reaction ticks | Attempt probability | Maximum held ticks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 24 | .20 | 24 |
| Normal | 12 | .45 | 36 |
| Hard | 8 | .70 | 48 |

Only an already-visible attack can start observation. Its instance must remain
observable for the configured delay before one random decision. Range/height,
facing and legal state are checked at decision time. There is no future input,
direct movement, free turn, stat change or guaranteed successful defense.
Fast attacks often beat reaction plus startup, especially on Easy.
A separate seeded RNG stream leaves offensive randomness untouched when no
defensive action intervenes. Existing offensive spacing, pursuit, facing,
reaction rearming and legal combo cancellation remain.

## Presentation and architecture
`src/core/defense.ts` contains configuration, validation, eligibility and fixed-tick
defense transitions. `combat.ts` owns contact outcomes, chip, interruption and
knockback. MatchInput supplies held block intent; MatchController owns resets.
None imports Phaser or uses wall-clock timers.

Original shield arcs follow the existing FighterView anchor/depth. Dim arcs mean
startup/recovery; solid arcs mean active block/blockstun. Distinct blue block
sparks and red guard-break fragments are replaceable procedural effects.
A compact labeled guard progress bar/status sits below each health bar.
Debug includes guard/break values; it does not mutate simulation.
Existing movement/jump anchors, projection and aerial steering remain.
Effects are read-only render consumers; no animation drives collision.

## Validation and limitations
Keep all existing regression files and browser checks. New coverage exercises
startup/recovery, blockstun, front/rear/side/overlap, chip and KO, guard costs for
every attack class, regeneration, non-refreshing breaks with early/late follow-ups,
blocked combo denial, illegal states, walls/corners, CPU delays/seeds and legal
slow-attack defense, help/focus/held release, match lifecycle and equal full
defensive traces at 10/30/60/144/240 Hz.

Compiled Chromium/Firefox/WebKit use shipped keyboard L and public HUD/Debug to
observe block, chip/guard depletion and guard break, plus help/blur rearming and
new-match reset. They retain the player 4-hit / 34-damage combo and complete
existing match/layout flows, without health/position/score/controller mutation.
The original development Chromium suite remains too.

Use the PR and CI run for final exact-head results. Locally available Node 24
differs from pinned Node 22.22.0; GitHub CI uses the pinned version. Desktop
browser automation is not real-device, mobile gameplay or FPS certification.
Human guard readability, chip lethality, CPU difficulty and combo balance remain
pending. Existing large Phaser bundle warning remains visible.

Do not merge or deploy this feature without approval.
