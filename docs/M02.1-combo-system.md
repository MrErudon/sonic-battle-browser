# M02.1 — Combo system and combat feel

## Baseline and scope
Based on merged M01.6 main `d9059d28a93676b912df6649ac856d569c9eeac3`.
No change to movement, arena projection, match scoring, deployment configuration,
standalone heavy, or aerial attack statistics. Exact tested head and CI outcomes
are recorded in PR #7. No merge or deployment is authorized.

J starts Light 1. Fresh J taps chain Light 2 then Light 3. Fresh J or K after
Light 3 starts the heavy finisher. K from neutral remains standalone heavy.
Only ground, hit-confirmed recovery cancels are supported. No automatic turn,
dash, homing, pursuit, jump cancel or air chain is added.

## Tick and input contract
An attack starts on frame 1. Existing timers advance before movement and
continuation evaluation. Phase ranges are inclusive: Light 1 startup 1–5,
active 6–8, recovery 9–18. Its first legal cancel is frame 9. On a successful
cancel, successor starts at frame 1 and does not advance again that tick.

Inputs consumed on a stage's tick can enter its one-slot continuation buffer.
A captured edge is eligible on its capture tick and five following ticks.
Thus a Light 1 input captured at frame 4 can execute at frame 9; capture at
frame 3 expires after frame 8. First eligible input wins; unsupported inputs
neither occupy nor replace the slot. Repeated keydown is suppressed by MatchInput.

Cancels require the current attack's hit-target set to be nonempty, a configured
successor, a live buffered edge and the inclusive cancel window. A whiff cannot
chain. A new attack gets a fresh hit-target set. Facing is inherited across
the chain. Valid cancels bypass predecessor recovery/cooldown; terminal or dropped
attacks complete ordinary recovery/cooldown. No buffer persists into neutral,
hitstun, KO, a replacement attack or a different round. A fresh legal neutral
press can still start an attack after recovery and cooldown; this is not a stale
continuation. No general neutral/hitstun/cooldown/landing input buffering exists.

All contacts are gathered before damage, preserving simultaneous trades.
Recovery cancels start in startup; an incoming active hit can interrupt the
new successor on that same tick. The interrupted action and its pending buffer
are discarded. Landing clears pending continuation; aerial attacks retain their
existing completion and reduced steering behavior.

Focus/help changes clear both edge capture and attack-instance buffers; held
keys remain governed by existing release/repress rules. Entering match states,
new rounds, menu, KO adjudication and rematch clear combo feedback and pending
inputs. Help/blur do not cancel an already-running attack or alter combat clocks.

## Data and validation
`src/core/combos.ts` defines finite forward transitions and partial stat overrides.
`FighterStats.combo` permits per-fighter configuration; absent configuration uses
defaults derived from the fighter's ground light/heavy definitions. Validators
reject duplicate IDs, missing/backward/cyclic/skipped transitions, invalid
durations/dimensions, nonfinite values and cancels outside recovery. Maximum four
stages, one terminal stage and buffer length 1–60 ticks. Each stage must leave a
neutral-restart gap longer than its hitstun, measured from its final active tick.
The default six-tick buffer and approved windows are unchanged.

## Escape and feedback
Earliest-hit to latest-cancel next-hit gaps are 10/12/17 ticks versus
12/14/18 hitstun for the three transitions. Connections require real geometry;
near range edges, knockback may cause a later attack to miss.
Finite chains cannot cancel backward or from finisher to entry. A missed
continuation does not retain stage progress. Uncanceled recovery and cooldown
create escape time even where walls suppress separation. Wall/corner tests
exercise repeated legal attack intents and require both damage and control gaps.

The hit counter tracks one attacker/defender pair and actual registered damage,
not button presses or attack stages. It increments only if the defender was
still in hitstun at the beginning of the hit tick. Otherwise it restarts at one.
Getting hit clears the attacker's feedback, including simultaneous trades.
The HUD starts at two hits; final display remains at most 45 simulation ticks
after the last hit. Display persistence does not preserve combo eligibility.
KO and round/menu/rematch clear it. Simulation counters remain independent of FPS.

Finisher uses larger amber impact spokes; Light 3 uses cyan spokes; other hits
retain white spokes. These original procedural effects are replaceable. No
hitstop, camera shake or animation-driven hit detection is introduced.
Visual state maps to Chat 03 attack_1/attack_2/attack_3/attack_heavy concepts;
authored sheets and a manifest are not part of this milestone.

## CPU
M01.4 horizontal spacing, height waiting, legal facing corrections and reaction
rearming remain. A separate once-per-attack decision allows continuation only
after a hit and inside the player's cancel window. It emits an ordinary light
edge; combat remains the sole authority for accepting it. No movement/stat
mutation or opponent-input reading is used. Continuation chance is configurable
as CpuConfig.comboChance: Easy .25, Normal .60, Hard .85, custom default .60.
Choices use the existing local seeded RNG. Initiation/standalone intervals stay
unchanged; legal internal cancels intentionally bypass the initiation interval.
Prior exact random traces can differ because combo decisions consume RNG draws.

## Validation
The full existing suite is retained, including five-seed M01.4 anti-oscillation,
jumping starvation, overlap, hitstun and knockback cases. The CPU regression's
old unconditional busy-state assertion is narrowed only for provably legal
hit-confirmed recovery cancels. No damage/attack bounds are loosened.

New tests cover stage boundaries, six-tick lifetime, whiffs, expiry, interruption,
heavy finisher inputs, full 34-damage chain, feedback, aerial/standalone behavior,
wall/corner control gaps, CPU inputs, input boundaries and equal combo traces at
10/30/60/144/240 Hz. Existing scoring, double KO and rematch tests remain required.

Production-browser tests exercise a full player combo using keyboard events,
public Debug coordinates/frame readouts and the HUD, without game-state mutation.
Browser time is accelerated for reproducibility. All Chromium/Firefox/WebKit
checks run against compiled dist. Existing Chromium legal-match victories,
defeats and scene-restart/listener cleanup checks are retained.
CI also runs clean npm ci, TypeScript, build, bundle reporting and full npm audit.
Screenshots/results are CI artifacts. Execution uses Actions because the local
shell/network cannot install and run this checkout.

Limitations: ground-only chaining, provisional balance, no guarantee of hits
at maximum range, no air/jump cancels, no guard, no hitstop or camera shake.
Frame-rate tests describe simulation equivalence, not low-end hardware FPS.
Independent review and human combo-feel playtesting remain required before merge.
